Governance & Dual-Class Voting Ring-Fencing

To secure unconditional, permanent founder control, equity is bifurcated into two explicit tracking classes:

  • Class A Shares (60% Allocation / 600 Billion Volume): Issued exclusively to Promoters. Carries 1 vote per share, commanding 100% of global voting control at General Meetings. Protected by a mandatory sunset provision; any external transfer instantly triggers a 1-for-1 automatic conversion into Class B Non-Voting equity.
  • Class B Shares (40% Allocation / 400 Billion Volume): Allocated to Foreign Institutional Investors (FII - 20%) and Public Market Allotments (IPO/FPO - 20%). Structurally stripped of all voting, meeting attendance, and notice privileges, save for explicit class variation or voluntary liquidation votes. Carries full, equal pari passu financial dividend and winding-up distribution rights.

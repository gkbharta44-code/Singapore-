1. Corporate Architecture & Network Design

Project Delper Ecom operates under a highly optimized Decentralized Operational / Centralized Intellectual Property (IP) corporate architecture designed to maximize legal insulation and eliminate global tax frictions.

The corporate layout is structured across three distinct tiers:

  • The Asset Lessor (GK Bharta): Individually holds 100% of the proprietary rights, source code, and master ERC-20 smart contract infrastructure for the Delper Token, leasing the assets via an exclusive commercial framework to the corporate operations.
  • The Central Apex (Singapore Holding Co.): Incorporated as a Private Limited company (Pte. Ltd.) in the Republic of Singapore, this unit acts as the exclusive global master lessee, ultimate clearinghouse, and treasury manager for the entire global network.
  • The Operational Ring (235 Global Subsidiaries): A decentralized footprint spanning 235 distinct jurisdictional entities. These units are legally organized as Non-Revenue Generating Data Compliance Representatives. They are pure corporate observers that hold no bank accounts, execute zero local commercial invoicing, and own no property title or physical logistics infrastructure. They exist solely for regional legal presence and domestic data compliance reporting.

2. The Unhedged Circular Flow & Economic Loop

The business process integrates digital advertising monetization with global e-commerce fulfillment into a closed-loop system governed entirely by the Singapore engine. The physical and digital flows operate through five seamless steps:

  • Stage 1 — Revenue Intake: High-margin programmatic advertising networks (Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin) pay 100% of their fiat revenues directly to the Singapore holding company natively denominated in Singapore Dollars (SGD).
  • Stage 2 — Digital Drawdown: Singapore allocates internal batches of the fixed 1-billion treasury supply of ERC-20 Delper Tokens down to the application's regional cloud layers based on tracked local ad engagement metrics.
  • Stage 3 — Micro-Rewarding: The global app backend programmatically credits the local ad viewer's user profile wallet. The token is programmatically locked within the ecosystem — visible on the app interface but incapable of being exported to public crypto wallets or external decentralized exchanges (DEXs).
  • Stage 4 — The Recycling Event: The user redeems their tokens at checkout to buy essentials delivered to their doorstep. The tokens leave the user's profile and transfer directly back to the master Singapore Corporate Treasury Wallet. The transaction loop closes, resetting the tokens to zero velocity for reuse.
  • Stage 5 — Global Vendor Settlement: Upon token recycling, the Singapore company draws from its liquid SGD ad cash reserves to settle wholesale invoices with global supply vendors and international logistics carriers. Goods are shipped directly from the factories and pass through international customs straight to the viewer's doorstep, leaving the local observer entities completely out of the financial flow.

3. Token Analytics & Dual-Currency Mechanics

The Delper Token functions as an internal, reusable corporate asset utility rather than an open speculative vehicle. To bridge real-world pricing stability with seamless treasury operations, the architecture utilizes a dual-currency translation matrix:

  • The Ledger Layer (USD Denomination): The underlying ERC-20 smart contract tracks all internal volume, user rewards, and transaction logs using a fixed USD accounting peg (e.g., 1 Delper Token = $0.10 USD static value). This provides standard, universally understood e-commerce value parity across international borders.
  • The Treasury Layer (SGD Clearing): The central Singapore server runs an automated translation node that processes every USD ledger event into physical Singapore Dollars using the real-time spot reference rate published by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).
  • Zero-Hedging Cost Victory: Because real-world cash inflows (Ad Revenue) and real-world cash outflows (Global Supplier Payments) are both executed exclusively in SGD at matching 1:1 values via the translation layer, the treasury incurs zero foreign exchange (FX) conversion spreads, bank fees, or currency hedging costs.

4. The 5% p.a. Lease Royalty Framework

Pursuant to the master asset lease, the Singapore holding company pays GK Bharta a continuous lease royalty calculated at an annualized rate of five percent per annum (5.00% p.a.) applied pro-rata to the asset value of every transaction that enters the recycling wallet.

  • Accrual and Settlement Loop: As tokens exit a user's wallet at checkout, the system computes the precise 5% p.a. pro-rata lease cost based on the transaction's duration in circulation or accumulated volume.
  • Because the token balances are logged in USD, the royalty balances accrue on the ledger in USD but are settled and wired to GK Bharta exclusively in SGD using the month-end closing conversion rate.
  • Accounting Status: Under IFRS 16 / SFRS(I) 16 guidelines, this payment is classified as a standard, predictable right-of-use corporate operating expense, making it fully deductible against the Singapore company's aggregated service revenues.

5. Legal Exemptions & Regulatory Immunities

By hardcoding absolute non-transferability and a strict ban on fiat cash-outs directly into the modified ERC-20 smart contract, the project achieves an unparalleled legal defense across all target markets:

  • Singapore (MAS Exemption): Because the token functions strictly within a closed loop as an internal platform loyalty reward, it is completely excluded from licensing requirements under the Payment Services Act (PSA). The company avoids the complex and time-consuming Digital Payment Token (DPT) licensing regime entirely.
  • India (VDA Tax Protection): Because the token can only be redeemed for platform discounts and cannot be traded or converted to cash, it qualifies for explicit statutory exclusions under the Income-tax Act. Indian users are 100% exempt from the flat 30% Virtual Digital Asset (VDA) profit tax, and the platform is completely free from implementing the burdensome 1% TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) micro-transaction logs.
  • 233 Remainder Markets (Geofencing Guardrails): For highly restrictive zones with total digital asset bans, the server deploys automated API and IP geofencing filters. The system programmatically strips out the word "Token" and the token interface for users in restricted regions, replacing the backend mechanics entirely with traditional, flat percentage fiat discount codes or e-commerce coupons.

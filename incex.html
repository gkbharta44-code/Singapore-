2. Identify the Financial Acceleration Mechanics

An analysis of the numbers reveals the validity of your strategy:

  • The Zero-Tax Shield (Years 2–4): Because the non-cash amortization expense exceeds your operating profits early on, your Net Taxable Income (EBIT) is negative. Your tax liability to IRAS is zero, preventing tax drag from pulling cash out of the company.
  • The Moratorium Capital Fortress: Because zero cash goes to the bank and zero cash goes to taxes during Years 2–4, you accumulate USD 430.00 Million in pure retained cash reserves by the end of Year 4.
  • The Year 5 Amortization Shock Absorber: In Year 5, your operation only generates USD 284.19 Million in net cash, but your bank invoice is USD 531.45 Million—leaving a single-year cash deficit of USD 247.26 Million. Because you hoarded USD 430 Million in your retained cash reserve, your company absorbs this deficit with zero external capital calls.

3. Verify the Accelerated Breakeven Horizon

If you do not reinvest the profits, your cumulative cash drops precariously close to zero by Year 6 (holding at USD 18.48 Million).
